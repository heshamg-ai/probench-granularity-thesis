

Looking at the `to_numpy` method in `pandas/core/base.py`, I need to optimize the case when converting to NumPy float dtype with `na_value=np.nan`. The current implementation copies values, fills NAs, then converts to dtype - resulting in multiple data passes and potential extra copies.

The optimization will:
1. Detect when target dtype is float and na_value is np.nan
2. Use `np.where` to combine dtype conversion and NA filling in a single operation
3. Avoid extra copies when not needed

Looking at the `DataFrame.astype` method in `pandas/core/generic.py`, the optimization target is the branch that handles extension dtypes. The current implementation converts each column individually using a list comprehension and then concatenates the results, which is slow due to per-column Series overhead.

The optimization replaces the list comprehension + `concat` approach with a direct column-by-column conversion on the underlying manager, similar to how the dict-like dtype branch works. This avoids the overhead of constructing intermediate Series objects and concatenating them.

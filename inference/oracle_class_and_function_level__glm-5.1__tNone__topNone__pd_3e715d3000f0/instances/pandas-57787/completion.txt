Looking at the optimization request, the goal is to ensure that `Categorical.categories` preserves/returns `RangeIndex` when possible instead of converting it to a regular `Index`.

The `categories` property in `Categorical` simply returns `self.dtype.categories`. The categories are stored inside `CategoricalDtype`. Looking at how categories are set, the `_set_categories` method creates a new `CategoricalDtype` which, in its constructor, validates the categories through `CategoricalDtype._validate_categories`. This validation method currently casts any `RangeIndex` into a plain `Index`, losing the memory-efficient `RangeIndex` representation.

The fix is to update `_validate_categories` in `CategoricalDtype` to pass through `RangeIndex` objects without converting them, since `RangeIndex` is a valid subclass of `Index` that already satisfies all the uniqueness and null-value constraints required for categories.

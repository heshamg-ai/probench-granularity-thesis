Looking at the optimization request, I need to optimize `DataFrame.join` when `sort=True` for left/right joins. The core issue is in `Index._join_monotonic` which is called during the join process. When `sort=True` and we have a left or right join with a unique other index, the result of `_left_indexer_unique` returns the right indexer (which maps left positions to right positions), but the join index is set to `self` (the left index). When sorting is needed later, this left index gets sorted, but the right indexer isn't reordered correspondingly, causing incorrect results.

The fix: when the left index isn't monotonic (i.e., needs sorting), sort it first and compute the right indexer against the already-sorted index. This avoids the downstream sorting issue and produces a correctly ordered result in one pass.
